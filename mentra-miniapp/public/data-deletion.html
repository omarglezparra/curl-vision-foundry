Remove stored workout information

Delete from the signed-in MiniApp

  1. Open AI Javier Coach from the Mentra app.
  2. Stop any active workout recording.
  3. At the bottom of the workout screen, choose Delete my data.
  4. Confirm the irreversible deletion prompt.

This deletes raw captures, derived workout rows, manifests, saved stream state, and saved camera-orientation preferences associated with the MiniApp's pseudonymous profile identifier. It does not delete your Mentra account or information controlled independently by Mentra.

If you need help

Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} for help using the in-app deletion control. Workout storage uses a pseudonymous identifier and does not keep an email-to-profile lookup, so an email address alone cannot identify data for deletion. We will never ask for your password or API key.

Automatic deletion

Azure lifecycle rules make raw-capture blobs eligible for automatic deletion {{RAW_CAPTURE_RETENTION_DAYS}} days after their last modification. Scheduled processing removes individual derived workout rows when their capture is older than {{DERIVED_DATA_RETENTION_DAYS}} days; shared derived files also have an Azure lifecycle backstop when they stop being updated. Azure processes deletions asynchronously. A failed-transfer segment may remain on the restricted production ingest host for up to 24 hours; it is discarded immediately when Azure reports that its profile was deleted. A deletion tombstone containing only the pseudonymous profile identifier and deletion time may remain for up to {{DERIVED_DATA_RETENTION_DAYS}} days to stop delayed uploads from restoring deleted data. Limited security logs may remain for the hosting provider's configured log-retention period.
