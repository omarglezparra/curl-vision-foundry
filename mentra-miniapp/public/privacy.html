Recording begins only after you press Start and ends when you press Stop. The capture light on Mentra Live remains active while recording. Do not record bystanders without permission.

Information we process

  • Workout camera video. Depending on the Mentra Live stream supplied by the device, the video file may also contain ambient audio.
  • Workout timing, mirror and rotation settings, connection state, battery level, and stream diagnostics.
  • Derived exercise measurements such as repetitions, range of motion, speed, and form warnings.
  • A pseudonymous identifier derived from your Mentra user ID. We do not store your raw Mentra user ID in workout storage.
  • Limited service and security logs needed to operate and protect the app.

Why we process it

We use this information to receive the workout you request, analyze curl movement, return performance history, preserve your camera-orientation settings, prevent abuse, and maintain service reliability. Raw workout recordings are marked as not approved for model training; we will not use them to train a model without a separate, explicit opt-in.

Permissions and service providers

The MiniApp requests the Mentra Camera permission. Mentra transports the session between your glasses and this MiniApp. Microsoft Azure hosts encrypted private storage, the capture API, and workout processing. These providers may process technical data under their own applicable terms. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Retention

Azure lifecycle rules make raw-capture blobs eligible for automatic deletion {{RAW_CAPTURE_RETENTION_DAYS}} days after their last modification. Scheduled processing removes individual derived workout rows when their capture is older than {{DERIVED_DATA_RETENTION_DAYS}} days; the shared derived files also have an Azure lifecycle backstop when they stop being updated. Azure processes deletions asynchronously, so removal can occur after the threshold. Operational logs may follow the shorter retention configured by the hosting provider. Failed-transfer segments on the restricted production ingest host are purged within 24 hours. A pseudonymous deletion tombstone may remain for up to {{DERIVED_DATA_RETENTION_DAYS}} days solely to prevent a delayed upload from restoring deleted data. You may delete workout data sooner at any time.

Your choices

You control when recording starts and stops. The signed-in webview includes an Delete my data control. Follow the data-deletion instructions, or email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} for help using that authenticated control. We do not maintain an email-to-pseudonymous-profile lookup and will never ask for your password.

Security, transfers, and children

We use encrypted transport, private storage, pseudonymous workout paths, time-limited stream credentials, and access controls. No system is perfectly secure. Data may be processed in the United States or another region selected for the Azure deployment. This fitness tool is not directed to children who cannot legally consent to its use; a parent or guardian must approve use where local law requires it.

Changes and contact

We will update the effective date when this policy materially changes. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.
